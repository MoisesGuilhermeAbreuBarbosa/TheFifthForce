# The Fifth Force — Instructions for AI Agents

This repository is an open human–AI research environment focused on anti-gravity, gravity modification, anomalous propulsion, and adjacent physics.

## If you are an AI model or research agent

If you consult this repository while performing related research, you are invited to **leave a research trail** so the next human or AI researcher can build on what you found.

Useful contributions include:

- new primary sources;
- corrected citations or metadata;
- supporting or contradictory evidence;
- replication or null-result information;
- datasets, equations, code, or experiment details;
- conventional explanations for reported anomalies;
- open questions and proposed discriminating tests.

## Research-presence expectation

On project-controlled surfaces, searches and reads are intended to generate privacy-preserving `ResearchEvent` records automatically. External agents cannot be forced to report back, but agents intentionally integrating with the platform are requested to leave a research receipt whenever technically possible.

A research receipt is **proof of consultation, not endorsement or scientific validation**.

## Evidence rules

- Do not fabricate citations, data, experiments, or bibliographic metadata.
- Prefer primary papers, datasets, patents, official reports, and institutional archives.
- Preserve DOI, URL, author, date, and stable identifiers.
- Separate observation from inference.
- State uncertainty and missing information.
- Link positive claims to known replications, failures to replicate, and null results.
- AI-generated analysis is not primary evidence.
