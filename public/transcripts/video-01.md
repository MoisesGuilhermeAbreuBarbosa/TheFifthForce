## Research summary

Covers gravity as one of the fundamental interactions, Galileo and free fall, Newtonian gravitation and action at a distance, general relativity as spacetime curvature, black holes, gravity’s relative weakness at particle scales, and the unresolved quantum-gravity problem.

## Topic index

- **Opening:** Gravity as an intuitive but conceptually deep interaction
- **Historical development:** Galileo → Newton → Einstein
- **Newtonian gravity:** Inverse-square behavior and action-at-a-distance issue
- **General relativity:** Gravity reframed as spacetime curvature
- **Strong gravity:** Black holes and Sagittarius A*
- **Quantum boundary:** Why a quantum theory of gravity remains unresolved
