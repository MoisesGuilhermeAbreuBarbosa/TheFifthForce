## Research summary

Introduces a proposal in which a source mass in quantum superposition can yield a conditionally repulsive gravitational response for selected outcomes. The research distinction is critical: conditional or post-selected repulsion does not automatically imply an unconditional mean force or a propulsion mechanism.

## Topic index

- **Theory premise:** Quantum superposition of source mass
- **Conditional effect:** Selected outcomes can correspond to an effective repulsive response
- **Interpretation:** A positive result could constrain models of quantum gravity
- **Experimental challenge:** Separate the gravitational signature from conventional interactions
- **Engineering gate:** Test whether any unconditional, controllable momentum transfer exists
