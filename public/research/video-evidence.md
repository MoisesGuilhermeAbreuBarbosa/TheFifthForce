## Follow the paper behind the headline

[Saldanha, Marletto and Vedral, arXiv:2602.12266](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12266) proposes a gravitational test using source superposition and postselection. The proposal is not an experimental detection. Repository review should calculate outcome probabilities, the unconditioned mean, decoherence and non-gravitational backgrounds before discussing a device. Its postselection mechanism is a different research direction from the screened-vector model.
