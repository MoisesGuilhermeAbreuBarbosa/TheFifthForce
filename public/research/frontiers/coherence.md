# Does a phase shift imply a force?

F05 · Proposed investigation · 2026-09-13

AI-assisted research design; not peer reviewed. No new experimental result.

## Research question
Which observable would distinguish a genuine additional interaction from ordinary unitary phase evolution or postselection? This dossier extends the existing coherence research program by defining observable-specific evidence requirements.

## Minimal quantum description
An effective two-body nonrelativistic Hamiltonian may be written

$$
\hat H=\frac{\hat p_A^2}{2m_A}+\frac{\hat p_B^2}{2m_B}+V_N(\hat r)+V_X(\hat r)+\hat H_{\rm environment}.
$$

For prescribed branch trajectories, a potential difference accumulates phase

$$
\Delta\varphi=-\frac{1}{\hbar}\int\Delta V(t)\,dt.
$$

A phase is not a measured acceleration. Momentum responds through

$$
\frac{d\langle\hat p\rangle}{dt}=-\langle\nabla V\rangle
$$

under the usual Hamiltonian assumptions. Interference contrast, relative phase, conditional centroid, unconditional centroid and an entanglement witness answer different questions.

## Selection accounting
If outcomes partition an ensemble, then

$$
\langle p\rangle=\sum_k P(k)\langle p\rangle_k.
$$

A large shift in a rare selected output can coexist with no shift in the unconditional ensemble. Retain the complementary outcomes and success probabilities. The identity should be checked before associating a selected displacement with thrust or gravity control.

## Proposed discriminating test
Prepare a fully specified two-branch model with controlled phase and a calibrated conventional force. Predict phase and centroid changes separately. Simulate environmental dephasing, imperfect detection and unequal selection efficiencies. Then compare measured joint statistics to explicit alternative models. A contrast-only readout is insufficient to certify every property of the intermediate state.

For gravity-mediated entanglement, state the local-operation, mediator and initial-correlation assumptions, identify the witness and calculate the ordinary electromagnetic contribution. Entanglement is a possible probe of the mediator under assumptions; it is not an anti-gravity mechanism. The linked historical comment and reply demonstrate why preparation and readout details matter.

## Acceptance and deliverables
Require normalization, probability conservation, selection accounting, a known-force calibration and an independent implementation of the predicted statistics. Release the Hamiltonian, state preparation, measurement operators, decoherence model and uncertainty budget. Label classical state-vector calculations as simulations. A new claim must outperform explicit conventional alternatives and survive the same analysis on the complementary outputs.

The existing coherence explorer is a useful starting benchmark. Extending it to a realistic experimental likelihood remains proposed work; no gravitational entanglement or anomalous force is reported here.
