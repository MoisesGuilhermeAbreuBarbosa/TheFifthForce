GRAVITY MODIFICATION / ENGINEERING WORKING PAPER 02

From Gravity Theory to a Laboratory Test

A preliminary engineering design for source-modulated atom interferometry · Revision 1.0 · 5 September 2026
Download editable DOCXOpen questions

Prepared for Moises Barbosa • 5 September 2026 • Revision 1.0

Status: research design, not a demonstrated anti-gravity machine or a fabrication release. Calculations below are idealized engineering estimates; no new experiment or scalar-field simulation has been performed for this paper.

Abstract and engineering decision

A practical program needs a measurable physical hypothesis before it needs a propulsion device. This paper specifies a laboratory instrument that measures the horizontal attraction of a movable source mass to freely falling rubidium atoms, subtracts a geometrically calculated Newtonian response, and searches for a source-correlated residual. The proposed dual-channel Raman interferometer uses ordinary laboratory technology. Its ambitious design targets are 10⁻⁹ m/s² statistical uncertainty and 2 × 10⁻¹⁰ m/s² systematic uncertainty after averaging. Neither target is demonstrated here.

A canonical screened scalar field is selected as a conditional test model because its equations yield a calculable laboratory signal. It is not selected as an established route to anti-gravity: with universal same-sign couplings its additional force is attractive. A null result constrains model parameters; an attractive residual would be a new-force candidate; neither would demonstrate gravitational repulsion. Published atom experiments already constrain these models [R1–R4].

1. Correcting the physics before building

Earlier inference or common error Correction and design consequence
Screened scalar fields are the strongest anti-gravity route. They are experimentally testable candidates for an additional force, not a demonstrated repulsive mechanism. Do not advertise this instrument as a levitator.
Dual-species subtraction is automatically optimal. A universal signal can cancel in co-located species differences. Use spatially separated, same-species channels for discovery; use species changes later as a diagnostic.
Vacuum or chamber walls can be omitted from the field model. Nonlinear screening depends on the density and boundaries of the entire apparatus. Include walls, source, supports and residual gas [R2].
Negative pressure alone specifies a buildable repulsive device. A finite apparatus requires a complete stress-energy distribution, boundary stresses, a consistent spacetime solution and stability analysis. A cosmological equation of state is not a construction prescription.
An apparent weight reduction establishes gravity modification. Electromagnetic forces, source-stage motion, tilt and thermal drift can imitate it. Measure freely falling probes, source geometry and environmental response; demand independent replication.

What would count as anti-gravity?

Define the target operationally: a controlled source produces an acceleration away from itself, attributable to gravity or a specified new universal interaction, after conventional forces and metrology artifacts have been bounded. Ordinary upward attraction toward a mass above a probe does not meet this definition. Electromagnetic levitation can support a body but does not reverse its gravitational coupling.

Gravity shielding is a separate hypothesis: an intervening device changes the response to an external mass after the device’s own Newtonian field is included. The baseline experiment does not test Earth-gravity shielding directly. Such a claim needs a separate source–shield–probe geometry and a quantitative shielding law.

Limit of scale arguments

A point-source estimate M = ar²/G gives about 1.47 × 10¹¹ kg for a = 9.81 m/s² at r = 1 m. This is an illustrative Newtonian mass requirement, not a universal bound on every proposed geometry. Likewise, vacuum-energy estimates for de Sitter-like models cannot be treated as bills of materials for finite laboratory devices. These corrections narrow unsupported claims; they do not supply a missing material or mechanism.

2. Conditional model and force-sign gate

For a canonical chameleon scalar, take a positive field φ, positive index n and matter coupling A(φ) = exp(βφ/Mₚ). In the following field equations use natural units ℏ = c = 1; Mₚ is the reduced Planck mass and ρ is nonrelativistic matter energy density. The gravitational background remains weak and approximately static. [R2, R5]

\[V(\phi)=\Lambda^4+\frac{\Lambda^{4+n}}{\phi^n},\qquad V_{\mathrm{eff}}(\phi)=V(\phi)+\rho A(\phi)\]
\[\nabla^2\phi=-n\frac{\Lambda^{4+n}}{\phi^{n+1}}+\frac{\beta}{M_P}\rho\,e^{\beta\phi/M_P}\]

For βφ/Mₚ ≪ 1 and positive β, the approximate equilibrium field in homogeneous matter is φmin = [nΛ⁴⁺ⁿMₚ/(βρ)]¹⁄⁽ⁿ⁺¹⁾. The local squared effective mass is the second derivative of Veff. Density-dependent field profiles can suppress the response of macroscopic objects; do not assign an unscreened charge to every atom or source without checking the selected parameter point.

\[a_\phi=-q c^2\nabla\ln A(\phi)\approx-qc^2\frac{\beta}{M_P}\nabla\phi\]

The acceleration expression is in SI when φ and Mₚ use the same energy units and the gradient is per metre; q is an effective screening factor only where that reduced description is valid. A fully resolved nonlinear source calculation should not then be multiplied by a second source-screening factor.

Why this does not predict repulsion

\[U(r)=-\frac{Gm_s m_t}{r}\left[1+\alpha e^{-r/\lambda}\right]\]
\[\alpha=2\beta_s\beta_t q_s q_t\ge0\]

In the linear weak-field regime the Yukawa correction is attractive for α ≥ 0. Screening reduces the effective response; it does not reverse that sign. Opposite couplings could change the sign, but are not the universal model above and would require a separately specified, stable theory and compatibility with composition tests. The Yukawa expression is a sign and limiting-case check, not a substitute for a nonlinear chamber solution. [R2, R5]

Model admission criteria

Start with n = 1 as a modeling case, not a favored truth. Scan Λ and β only after recording published bounds and their assumptions. Accept a science benchmark only if it is not already excluded under the same model and is distinguishable in this apparatus. If no such benchmark survives, stop the new-physics build or retain it explicitly as a metrology demonstrator. No viable benchmark is claimed in this revision.

3. Reference apparatus and interfaces

Reference geometry: two synchronized ⁸⁷Rb interferometers sense horizontal acceleration along x. The test cloud has its fountain apex at (0, 0, 0); the reference cloud is at (0, 0.20 m, 0). A solid aluminum sphere of radius 0.025 m is parked at (±0.035 m, 0, +0.030 m). Its bottom is 5 mm above the apex beam axis. Require a ≤2 mm vertical beam radius and verify clipping over both atom paths. The apex-to-surface gap is 21.1 mm. Full clearance review remains required.

Subsystem Preliminary specification / interface
Atom preparation Two synchronized laser-cooled ⁸⁷Rb ensembles; target 10⁵ detected atoms per channel per shot and contrast 0.30. Verify temperature, cloud extent and velocity distribution experimentally.
Interrogation Horizontal Raman beams near 780 nm; π/2–π–π/2 pulses at 0, T, 2T; T = 50 ms. Switch between lower (−12.3 mm) and apex beam paths. Share timing and a mechanically common retroreflection reference; calibrate each path’s phase and wavefront.
Vacuum and source UHV-compatible chamber accommodating both clouds, trajectories and beam access. Initial operating-pressure target ≤10⁻⁹ mbar, contingent on atom lifetime, outgassing and field modeling; not proof of force isolation.
Source assembly Weighed and surveyed 50 mm-diameter aluminum sphere on a remotely actuated vacuum-compatible carriage. Survey the complete moving assembly, not just the sphere.
Position metrology Independent source encoders plus optical survey of cloud trajectories; provisional relative source/cloud coordinate uncertainty ≤10 µm in the science fit.
Environmental sensing Mirror acceleration, platform tilt, magnetic field and gradients, temperatures, residual-gas pressure, source position, Raman power and frequency; timestamp with the shot record.
Control / data Balanced randomized source states and Raman directions; immutable raw shot records, calibration versions, state labels and blinded analysis offset.

Mechanical layout constraints

The source must transfer between sides only when no interrogation is occurring, with a retracted waypoint clear of atoms and beams. Lock out laser interrogation until measured vibration settling meets its allocation. Source mounts, bellows and cables can dominate the state-dependent Newtonian field or transmit motion; their CAD, density and surveyed position are required inputs. This is a functional layout, not an approved mechanical drawing.

4. Predicted signal and sensitivity

\[\Phi=k_{\mathrm{eff}}\int_0^{2T}w(t)a_x[r(t)]\,dt\]
\[k_{\mathrm{eff}}\approx\frac{4\pi}{780\,\mathrm{nm}}=1.611\times10^7\,\mathrm{m}^{-1}\]

At a vertical fountain apex midway through interrogation, z(t) = −g(t − T)²/2. The cloud lies 12.3 mm below the apex at the first and last pulses. The sphere’s horizontal field therefore varies during the shot; its apex value must not be used as the final prediction.

\[M=\frac{4\pi}{3}R^3\rho_{Al}=0.1767\,\mathrm{kg}\]
\[a_{x,N}(t)=\frac{GMx_s}{\left[x_s^2+y^2+(z(t)-z_s)^2\right]^{3/2}}\]

For xₛ = +0.035 m, the apex test-channel acceleration is 4.21 × 10⁻⁹ m/s². The reference also responds: its apex x acceleration is about 4.77 × 10⁻¹¹ m/s². This few-nanometres-per-second-squared signal is a Newtonian calibration target, not an anomalous-force prediction. Integrate both trajectories and every moving part for the actual baseline.

Signed estimator

\[k_{\mathrm{eff}}\approx\frac{4\pi}{780\,\mathrm{nm}}=1.611\times10^7\,\mathrm{m}^{-1}\]

Here phase signs have first been normalized for Raman direction, and s denotes source side. Define positive D as attraction toward the switched source. Fit the residual D − DN with geometry and environmental nuisance parameters. A negative residual alone is not proof of net repulsion: it may mean less attraction than modeled.

Ideal statistical floor—not an achieved specification

\[k_{\mathrm{eff}}\approx\frac{4\pi}{780\,\mathrm{nm}}=1.611\times10^7\,\mathrm{m}^{-1}\]

Assuming independent projection-limited channels, equal populations in the two source states, no additional noise and N total accepted paired shots, σD = σd/√N. Reaching 10⁻⁹ m/s² requires about 1.37 × 10⁵ shots. At an assumed one-second cycle this is 38 hours of accepted data, before settling, reversals, calibration and rejected shots. The source-motion cycle may be much slower. Correlated drift can invalidate the √N extrapolation; measure Allan deviation and block stability.

5. Field solver and engineering verification

No scalar-field forecast is reported here. Before hardware procurement, implement a 3D finite-element or finite-volume solution for the nonlinear boundary-value problem. Chamber geometry materially affects screening; published work provides an essential validation reference [R2].

Stage Required output and acceptance test
Geometry and units Versioned CAD-to-density map including source, support, chamber walls, windows and gas. Convert SI mass density to natural-unit energy density explicitly; unit-test conversions with a dimensional reference.
Boundary treatment Model dense walls and external environment. Derive exterior equilibrium conditions from the assumed ambient density. Enlarge the outer domain until the predicted differential phase changes by <1%.
Nonlinear solution Solve both source states; use positivity-preserving variables or continuation where needed. Record residuals, mesh and solver tolerances; reject nonconverged solutions.
Verification Recover homogeneous φmin, zero gradient in uniform equilibrium, and an analytic weak-field Yukawa limit. Refine mesh until the observable changes by <1%; independently reproduce one published geometry.
Atom response Propagate the measured cloud distribution and evaluate the interferometer phase. Resolve atomic screening or justify its approximation; compare point-probe and finite-cloud results.
Science reach Fit simulated data with surveyed-geometry priors and measured noise covariance. Compare confidence regions with current exclusions using identical model conventions.

Minimal computational sequence

For each admitted parameter point: create the density map for each source state; solve for φ; compute Newtonian gravity from the full mass map; evaluate both channel phases over their trajectories; apply the same estimator used for data; perturb geometry and environmental inputs; store predicted signal and covariance. Preserve input hashes and solver versions with each output.

Required completion package before fabrication release

Before fabrication release, deliver reviewed CAD and optical layouts, clearance and UHV checks, dummy-rig settling tests, wiring/timing diagrams, safety approval, field forecasts and vendor quotations. Numerical tolerances above are proposed, not achieved; tighten them wherever the measurement budget requires.

The following are proposed equivalent-acceleration uncertainty allocations for the final source-switched estimator, not measured performance. Units in the middle column are 10⁻¹⁰ m/s². Statistical uncertainty is budgeted separately at 10 units.

Source-correlated vibration / tilt 1.0 Dummy-motion tests, mirror sensors, measured settling, tilt transfer function; include Earth-gravity projection.
Wavefront / trajectory coupling 0.8 Wavefront mapping, cloud imaging, velocity and temperature scans; independently calibrate each optical path.
Magnetic gradients 0.5 Magnetically insensitive mF = 0 states, bias-field scans, map quadratic Zeeman shifts and source susceptibility.
Differential light shifts 0.5 Raman intensity and detuning scans; pulse-power logging, beam reversal and extrapolation.
Newtonian mass / geometry model 0.3 Weigh and survey the complete moving assembly; propagate correlated position and density uncertainties.

Rotation / transverse velocity 0.4 Measure launch velocity and Coriolis response; reverse appropriate launch offsets.
Unallocated reserve 0.8 Retain until commissioning identifies omitted couplings.

If genuinely independent, these entries combine to 1.78 × 10⁻¹⁰ m/s² in quadrature, below the 2 × 10⁻¹⁰ target. Their linear sum is 4.7 × 10⁻¹⁰ m/s². Use measured covariance or a conservative correlated bound; independence must not be assumed to make a budget pass.

Two demanding cross-checks

A source-correlated uncompensated tilt produces approximately gδθ along a horizontal axis. An allocation of 10⁻¹⁰ m/s² would require δθ ≈ 10⁻¹¹ rad without common-mode rejection. That is a severe requirement, not a credible passive-alignment specification. Demonstrate the dual-channel rejection transfer function and residual calibration experimentally before claiming feasibility.

For the sphere-only apex estimate, a 10 µm radial error at fixed direction gives roughly 2aδr/r = 1.83 × 10⁻¹² m/s². This does not validate the whole mass model: independent coordinate errors, carriage movement, reference response, trajectory drift and density variations must also be included.

7. Experimental protocol and inference

Commissioning and control sequence

Commission each interferometer without source motion. Measure contrast, phase noise, detection linearity and response to deliberately applied mirror acceleration. Commission both channels together and measure frequency-dependent vibration rejection. Then move an inert dummy carriage to quantify motion-correlated phase and settling before adding the calibrated source.

Model fit and stopping rule

Dᵢ = DN(geometryᵢ) + Dφ(parameters, geometryᵢ) + Σⱼ bⱼXᵢⱼ + εᵢ

Pre-register the source configurations, fit model, nuisance priors, exclusions, analysis cuts and maximum data-taking exposure. Estimate correlated noise from control runs; use a covariance-aware likelihood. Propagate survey uncertainty rather than subtracting a fixed perfect Newtonian value. Calibrate interval coverage with simulated data when parameters lie on boundaries or noise is non-Gaussian.

Report parameter confidence regions for the specific model and geometry. A candidate anomaly must survive held-out blocks, alternative reasonable analyses and deliberate interventions on likely artifacts. Use a predeclared discovery threshold—for example a globally corrected 5σ criterion—as one gate, not as a substitute for systematic-error review or independent replication. Do not stop opportunistically when a favorable fluctuation appears.

Data product for reproducibility

For every shot retain timestamp, source state and measured coordinates, Raman direction, pulse timing, raw atom counts and detection calibration, fitted phase, cloud state, mirror/tilt channels, laser diagnostics, temperature, pressure and rejection flags. Publish analysis code, complete mass model, numerical-field inputs, uncertainty budget and negative results. Record all post-unblinding changes.

Interpreting the outcome

Agreement with Newtonian gravity validates metrology and constrains admitted models. Extra attraction may support a new-force hypothesis only after controls. Reduced attraction warrants investigation, not an anti-gravity label. Net acceleration away from the source requires a different repulsive model and a new verification campaign.

8. Development plan and anti-gravity gates

Gate / indicative effort Pass condition If it fails
G0: theory and simulation; 2–4 months A stable, not-already-excluded benchmark produces a resolvable signal in the full chamber model; independent theory review. Do not claim new-physics reach; redesign or limit scope to calibration research.
G1: subsystem feasibility; 3–6 months Atom contrast and accepted-shot rate support the noise forecast; dummy source motion passes settling and common-mode tests. Revise geometry or interrogation method before completing the chamber.

Durations are overlapping planning assumptions, excluding procurement delays. Required expertise: atomic physics, UHV/mechanics, metrology/controls and numerical field theory. No vendor quotation or funded schedule is implied.

What must happen before an actuator program

Specify a stable, experimentally allowed repulsive interaction. Reproduce its outward acceleration with multiple probe materials; map range and controllability. Bound conventional couplings and measure source/support reactions, including supplies, fields and environmental momentum exchange in laboratory energy–momentum accounting.

Only then study acceleration per source mass, power, duty cycle, thermal load, bandwidth, payload scaling and fail-safe behavior. An attractive scalar discovery would not pass the repulsion gate. Section 2 provides no justified direct path from this model to a repulsive actuator.

Safety and release boundary

Require institutional safety review for lasers, interlocks, vacuum/bakeout, reactive alkali sources, electrical supplies and moving-source hazards. Commission only under approved procedures and component ratings—not from this paper alone. No exotic-energy source is required.

Conclusion

The implementable result is a precision experiment with falsifiable hypotheses and explicit stop conditions. A working anti-gravity device remains an unresolved physics objective. The next technically justified deliverable is a validated chamber-field forecast and subsystem feasibility review—not a claimed repulsive machine.
