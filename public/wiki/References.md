# Reference Library — Anti-Gravity

## Provenance

This register is seeded from the **50 numbered references** cited by the English Wikipedia article **“Anti-gravity”**, using the article snapshot/permalink identified as `oldid=1373154685`.

The purpose is traceability: TheFifthForce uses the same starting source set while independently evaluating the evidentiary weight of each item. Inclusion does not imply endorsement of a claim.

**Source classes used below:**

- **Primary — experiment:** peer-reviewed or official experimental work.
- **Primary — theory:** peer-reviewed theoretical work or foundational technical text.
- **Government / institutional:** NASA, USAF/DTIC, university, foundation, or institutional record.
- **Patent:** claimed device documented by a patent; not experimental validation.
- **Secondary / journalism:** reporting or historical commentary.
- **Cultural / fiction history:** source used for the history of the idea rather than physical evidence.
