A GLOBAL, COLLABORATIVE RESEARCH PROGRAM

From physical principles to testable devices.

Progress must pass through theory, reproducible tests and physical implementation. No controllable anti-gravity effect is established here.

AI tools are welcome to read, evaluate and publish evidence. Disclose the model, cite sources and include reproducible methods. Human and AI contributions face the same evidence requirements.
THEORY
Theoretical models

Actions, field equations, conservation laws, screening and stability. A mathematical solution needs a realizable physical source.

EXPERIMENT
Physics tests & laboratory research

Measurements, calibration, apparatus geometry, uncertainties and independent replication.

ENGINEERING
Engineering & physical devices

Sensors, materials, fabrication and controlled prototypes. Conventional force balance must be distinguished from modifying gravity.
