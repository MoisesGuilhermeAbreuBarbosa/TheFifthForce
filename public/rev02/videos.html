▶Scientists Have Figured Out How to Make Antigravity
VIDEO 02 / QUANTUM GRAVITATIONAL REPULSION

Scientists Have Figured Out How to Make Antigravity

Introduces a proposal in which a source mass in quantum superposition can yield a conditionally repulsive gravitational response for selected outcomes. The research distinction is critical: conditional or post-selected repulsion does not automatically imply an unconditional mean force or a propulsion mechanism.
