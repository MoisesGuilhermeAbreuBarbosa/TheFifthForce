# The Fifth Force — Wiki

This directory is the research wiki for **TheFifthForce**, an evidence-oriented repository on anti-gravity, gravity modification, anomalous propulsion claims, and related physics.

## Core pages

- [Anti-Gravity](Anti-Gravity.md) — scientific overview, historical programs, experimental claims, null results, and evidence status.
- [Reference Library](References.md) — source register seeded from the references used by the English Wikipedia article **“Anti-gravity”**, snapshot/permalink oldid `1373154685`.

## Editorial policy

The wiki distinguishes between:

1. **Established physics** — results consistent with reproducible experimental evidence and mainstream gravitational theory.
2. **Primary experimental evidence** — peer-reviewed or official experimental reports, including positive and null results.
3. **Theoretical proposals** — mathematically formulated ideas that may not have experimental support.
4. **Historical or technical claims** — patents, program reports, historical accounts, and claimed devices.
5. **Secondary / popular sources** — journalism, books, archived web pages, and historical commentary.
6. **Fiction / cultural material** — included for history of the concept, not as physical evidence.

A citation appearing in this wiki does **not** imply that the underlying claim is accepted. Each page should state the evidentiary status of a claim separately from the fact that the claim was made.

## Source policy

The initial reference library intentionally mirrors the source set cited by the English Wikipedia **Anti-gravity** article so that claims can be traced to the same underlying literature while the text here remains independently written. Wherever possible, primary literature, DOI records, NASA/NTRS, DTIC, journal archives, and institutional sources should be preferred when extending the wiki.
